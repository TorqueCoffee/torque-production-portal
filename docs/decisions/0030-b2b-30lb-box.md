# 0030 — B2B packs into 30 lb boxes (18x14x14), 20 lb and under in the 14x10x10

- **Status**: Superseded by #0031 (Uline shipped the 20x15x10; cap raised to 40 lb)
- **Date**: 2026-10-20

## Context

The B2B packer had one carton: the 14x10x10, capped at 20 lb net. The cap was the USPS cubic ceiling and stayed under UPS (ADR 0020). Under UPS, weight above 20 lb no longer costs a box of its own, so a 30 lb order went out as two boxes and two labels.

Andy added a second carton: Uline S-4191, 18 x 14 x 14", 200 lb test, $3.01. Uline lists 32 lb per bundle of 20, so the tare is taken as 1.6 lb until a box is weighed.

TCCDS-40 (Cocoa Drops xBloom Bulk 40lb Box) ships as two 20 lb boxes. Andy: it stays that way for now, and will likely move to the 30 lb box later.

## Options considered

- **Fewest boxes at 30 lb, smaller carton for any box at 20 lb or less** — fewest labels; a light remainder still goes in the cheaper, smaller box. 6 x 5 lb is one 18x14x14; 8 x 5 lb is an 18x14x14 and a 14x10x10.
- **30 lb box only when the whole order fits in one, else 20 lb boxes** — fewer changes to large orders, but a 40 lb order stays at two boxes when one 30 and one 10 would do, and a 60 lb order is three boxes instead of two.
- **Cheapest by rate** — rate every split with Shippo. More calls per order and a pack that changes with rates; the label check (ADR 0025) needs a pack the server can rebuild without asking Shippo.

## Decision

First-Fit Decreasing with a 30 lb net cap. Then each box of 20 lb or less is labelled `14x10x10` and each heavier one `18x14x14`. A single unit over 30 lb is still unpackable. SKU overrides marked `solo` (TCCDS-40) get a 14x10x10 each and nothing is packed with them. The 18x14x14 tare is 1.6 lb in `CARTON_TARE_LB`. All in `lib/ship-rules.js`, so the page and the server's label check agree.

## Consequences

**Positive:** heavy orders need fewer boxes and labels. The pack is still pure and rebuilt on the server, and the signature carries the carton (`18x14x14@33`), so a page running the old packer is refused rather than trusted.

**Negative:** a full 30 lb box declares 33 lb (30.42 measured net + 1.6 tare + 0.05 = 32.07, rounded up). The 18x14x14's dimensional weight under UPS (18·14·14/139 ≈ 25.4 lb) is above the actual weight of a box holding 21–23 lb, so those boxes may bill at 26 lb. Fit is not modelled (as before): 30 lb of 12 oz bags is 40 bags, which the eyeball step must confirm. The tare is Uline's figure, not a weighed box.

**When to revisit:** after the first 18x14x14 is weighed packed (update the tare in `CARTON_TARE_LB` and `docs/carton_weights.csv`); when TCCDS-40 moves to the 30 lb box (drop `solo`, or change its override); if UPS invoices show the dim-weight billing makes 21–25 lb boxes cost more than two small ones.
