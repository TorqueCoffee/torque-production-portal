# 0031 — B2B large box is the 20x15x10, up to 40 lb (8 x 5 lb)

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-10-20
- **Supersedes**: [0030](./0030-b2b-30lb-box.md) (box size and cap; the packing method stays)

## Context

ADR 0030 added an 18x14x14 (Uline S-4191) at 30 lb. Uline shipped the 20 x 15 x 10" S-11378 instead. Uline lists the same 32 lb per bundle of 20, so the tare is again taken as 1.6 lb until a box is weighed.

A 5 lb bag is 11 x 7.5 x 6". The 14x10x10 already takes 4 bags in practice, and the 20x15x10 has the same height with room for two 14x10 floors side by side, so it should hold 8.

UPS Ground rates from Shippo on Torque's US UPS account, 2026-10-20, each box rated as its own shipment to a commercial address (San Francisco 94111, which carries a delivery-area surcharge, and Goleta 93117):

| Order | Old: 20 lb boxes | 30 lb cap (ADR 0030) | 40 lb cap, one box |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 x 5 lb | SF $29.77 / Goleta $26.97 | $21.14 / $20.76 | same |
| 6 x 5 lb | $29.73 / $28.91 | $22.49 / $21.22 | same |
| 8 x 5 lb | $36.84 / $36.80 | $33.80 / $31.73 | **$24.13 / $21.66** (43 lb declared) |

From 27 to 43 lb declared, the 20x15x10 goes up only $1–3; a second label costs $8.50–18. The 18x14x14 at 33 lb priced the same as the 20x15x10 but drew a UPS "Non-Standard Cube" alert. These are UPS reference rates; the invoice can differ.

## Options considered

- **20x15x10 at 30 lb** — only the label changes, but an 8-bag order stays at two labels (about $10 more each time).
- **20x15x10 at 40 lb** — 8 bags in one label; declared 43 lb, under UPS's 50 lb additional-handling line.
- **Higher than 40 lb** — a 9th bag declares about 49 lb, too close to 50 lb, and the box is full at about 8 anyway.

## Decision

The large B2B carton is `20x15x10` with a 40 lb net cap and a 1.6 lb tare. Boxes of 20 lb or less still go in the 14x10x10, and TCCDS-40 still ships as two solo 20 lb boxes. The 18x14x14 is removed from `CARTON_TARE_LB`, so a stale page that packs it gets no rate.

## Consequences

**Positive:** an 8-bag order ships as one label, at about $12.70 (SF) to $15.10 (Goleta) less than two 20 lb boxes.
**Negative:** a 43 lb box is heavy to lift. Fit is not modelled, and 8 bags is an estimate until someone packs one. The tare is Uline's figure.
**When to revisit:** after packing and weighing a full 20x15x10 (fit, and the tare in `CARTON_TARE_LB` and `docs/carton_weights.csv`); if UPS invoices differ from these rates; when TCCDS-40 moves to the large box.
